# 🏢 Data Centre Management System (DCMS)
### MIT World Peace University (MIT-WPU) × NIRVAA Solution Pvt. Ltd.

Comprehensive academic & operational management portal for M.Tech CSE (Data Centre Systems Engineering).

[![Deploy to Render](https://render.com/images/deploy-to-render-button.svg)](https://render.com/deploy?repo=https://github.com/pranavvvv555-maker/pranav_dcms)

---

## ⚡ Features
- **Student & Faculty Management:** Real-time directory, attendance tracking, and faculty profiles.
- **Timetable & Session Logs:** Automated session hour logging and class-rate tracking.
- **Assessments & Grading:** Term-work evaluations, grade sheets, and mock interview tracking.
- **Unit 2 Electrical Infrastructure Courseware:** Integrated lecture decks (PPTX), PDF study guides, and comprehensive exam notes available directly in the Download Center.
